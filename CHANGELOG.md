# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - unreleased

First version.

Hildir sells a horn. Sounded inside one of her three dungeons, it wakes the mini-boss you
already killed there, and nothing else in the dungeon changes.

Proven in all three - the Sealed Tower, the Howling Cavern and the Smouldering Tomb - and
in none of them does anything come back except the fight. Untested with more than one
player.

Two things worth knowing before you buy one. Hildir will not take a second quest chest, so
the cosmetic reward stays once per world however many times you fight for it. And the horn
is only taken from you if something actually wakes: if a boss is still alive, or somebody
has built inside the dungeon, it refuses and you keep it.
